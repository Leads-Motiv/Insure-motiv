Who qualifies

What the Pharmacy Needs to See

A specialist checks these with you on the phone.

Your coverage

  • An active Medicare prescription drug plan
  • A plan the pharmacy works with — a few carriers are not supported

Your medications

  • At least eight different prescription medications
  • Over-the-counter items do not count; prescribed inhalers, insulin, eye drops and vitamins do

Your situation

  • Able to make your own medical decisions, or someone with power of attorney on the call
  • Living at home rather than in a nursing home or assisted living facility
